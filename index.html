About the shop

A repair shop that stayed in one market.

FK
Fakhruddin KhanOwner, Air Cool India

I started this work in 2011 with a small toolbox and one rule I learned from my father: a machine is only as good as the line that feeds it. I still remember a summer when a customer's AC kept failing and the real fault was a choked pipe two feet behind it. We fixed that and the AC ran fine after.

Today the shop at Lekhraj Market handles air conditioners, refrigerators, geysers, RO purifiers, stabilizers, washing machines and water coolers. We keep spare parts in the shop, so many jobs do not wait for a part to arrive from somewhere else.

Come to the shop, call, or send a WhatsApp message. I would rather tell you honestly what a repair costs than surprise you later.
